Pharo News of the Week 43/2026
==============================

16 October - 23 October 2026


Highlights
----------

- files.pharo.org was down (fixed)
- VM: memory corruption in the allInstances primitive fixed on the
  main VM branch, with a backport for the Pharo 10 VM (#1115, #1116).
- Pharo 13: fix for a VM crash caused by stale FullBlockClosures
  after a class reshape (#20067).
- Pharo 15: more Calypso menus move to Commander2, and the pure
  Morphic find-and-replace dialog is removed in favour of the Spec
  one.
- PRs merged: pharo 9 (Pharo 15: 8, Pharo 13: 1), pharo-vm 2, Spec 0,
  NewTools 0, iceberg 0.

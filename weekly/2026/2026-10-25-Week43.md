Pharo News of the Week 44/2026
==============================
23 October - 30 October 2026

Highlights
----------
- Pharo 14 is released: 978 PRs and 616 closed issues since Pharo 13,
  the first release on the new autumn schedule. Tag v14.0.0 is on the
  Pharo14 branch; pharo.org downloads and zeroconf now point to
  Pharo 14.
    https://lists.pharo.org/hyperkitty/list/pharo-dev@lists.pharo.org/thread/IYZQGL3QXRCKCHJO3JB5XXOEPWSX2OG5/
- New settings model in Pharo 15: settings are now a UI-independent
  model loaded through SettingsManager; the old Morphic SettingBrowser
  and settings model are removed, with matching changes in NewTools
  and Iceberg.
- ZIP and compression overhaul: Zip64, ZipCrypto, symbolic links, zip
  slip protection, CRC checks for stored members, GZIP header fields
  and DEFLATE compression levels; the ZipPlugin primitives are removed.
- VM releases: pharo-vm v10.3.12 (pharo-10) and v12.0.6-beta
  (pharo-12).
- Merged PRs: pharo 8 (5 Pharo 15, 3 Pharo 14), pharo-vm 2, Spec 1,
  NewTools 3, Iceberg 2, pharo-website 4, pharo-launcher 1,
  pharo-zeroconf 1.
